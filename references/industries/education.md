# Education

## Primary User Goal

Learners, guardians, teachers, and administrators are trying to learn, submit, review, grade, schedule, or intervene under uneven time, device, bandwidth, language, and ability constraints.

## Trust Requirements

At the moment of decision, show course/class, learner identity, deadline, submission status, grading status, feedback source, attempt count, saved state, and whether an action is visible to teachers, guardians, or peers.

## Error Costs

High-cost mistakes include missed submission, wrong course submission, lost work, incorrect grade visibility, inaccessible content, or exposing learner information to the wrong audience.

## Density Expectations

Low to medium for learners; medium to high for teachers and administrators. Learning content needs readability and progress clarity; grading and roster views need compact comparison.

## Motion Budget

Low. Use motion sparingly for progress and feedback. Avoid gamified or celebratory motion where it distracts, shames, or creates inequity for reduced-motion users.

## Style Fit

Suitable: `playful-rounded`, `warm-organic`, `high-contrast-utility`, `swiss-grid`, `data-forward` for teacher analytics.

Conflicts: `luxury-minimal` when it hides affordances, `brutalist`, `retro-terminal` except coding contexts, and `glass-layered` where variable backgrounds reduce readability.

## Often Forgotten States

- Draft saved but not submitted
- Deadline passed with grace period
- Resubmission available or locked
- Offline draft queue
- Teacher feedback pending
- Accommodations or alternate format available
- Guardian/teacher/student role switch
- Low bandwidth media failure

## Domain-Specific Failure Modes

- Assignment says submitted when upload is still processing
- Draft is saved locally but not visible from another device
- Deadline timezone is ambiguous
- Quiz closes without warning while assistive technology user is navigating
- Feedback appears without explaining whether the grade is final
- Video lesson has no low-bandwidth or transcript fallback

## complianceConsiderations

This is orientation, not legal advice. Requirements vary by jurisdiction and must be verified with qualified legal, privacy, accessibility, institutional, and child-safety professionals.

Categories that typically apply include student privacy, child data protection, accessibility, records retention, consent, identity and role access, assessment integrity, and institutional policy.

Questions to ask a qualified professional:

- Which users are minors, and what consent or guardian access applies?
- What student records are protected and who may see them?
- Which accessibility standards govern course content and assessments?
- What retention, deletion, and export rules apply to submissions and grades?
- What accommodations must be supported without disclosing sensitive status?
