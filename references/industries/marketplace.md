# Marketplace

## Primary User Goal

Users are trying to evaluate another party, compare offers, transact, message, resolve issues, or maintain trust where inventory, identity, quality, and incentives vary.

## Trust Requirements

At the moment of decision, show seller/provider identity, verification status, reviews, price, fees, availability, terms, cancellation/refund policy, dispute path, and what platform protection applies.

## Error Costs

High-cost mistakes include choosing an unsafe or unsuitable provider, accepting misleading terms, paying outside protection, double-booking inventory, or missing dispute deadlines.

## Density Expectations

Medium density. Discovery can be visual; decision and checkout surfaces need compact comparison, trust signals, policy, and messaging context near the action.

## Motion Budget

Low to medium for discovery, low for booking/payment/dispute. Motion may help browse inventory but must not obscure trust signals or policy changes.

## Style Fit

Suitable: `high-contrast-utility`, `playful-rounded`, `warm-organic`, `data-forward`, `editorial` depending on category.

Conflicts: `brutalist`, `retro-terminal`, `luxury-minimal` when it hides trust details, `glass-layered` over listing media without solid surfaces, and `soft-neumorphic` for dispute or payment surfaces.

## Often Forgotten States

- Seller unavailable
- Listing stale or removed
- Verification pending or failed
- Review under moderation
- Payment held in escrow or pending
- Dispute opened, awaiting response, escalated, resolved
- Message delivery failed
- Availability conflict between search and checkout

## Domain-Specific Failure Modes

- Listing appears available but seller calendar is stale
- Review score lacks recency or count context
- Payment call-to-action does not explain platform protection boundary
- Provider verification badge has no explanation
- Dispute deadline is hidden in email only
- Search filters hide all results without showing which filter caused it
