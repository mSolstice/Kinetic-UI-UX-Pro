# Media Publishing

## Primary User Goal

Users are trying to read, watch, listen, subscribe, save, share, or publish while balancing attention, trust, source context, and paywall friction.

## Trust Requirements

At the moment of decision, show title, source, byline, date, update status, content type, paywall/subscription terms, ad/sponsored labels, correction status, and reading progress or save state.

## Error Costs

High-cost mistakes include misleading attribution, obscured corrections, inaccessible media, accidental subscription, lost reading position, or confusing sponsored content with editorial content.

## Density Expectations

Variable density. Reading surfaces need spacious rhythm; feeds, editorial dashboards, and archives need scannable metadata and filters.

## Motion Budget

Low to medium. Motion can support story transitions and media controls, but reading stability wins. Avoid scroll effects that move text, interrupt reading, or hide controls.

## Style Fit

Suitable: `editorial`, `swiss-grid`, `luxury-minimal`, `warm-organic`, `glass-layered` only for media chrome with solid text surfaces.

Conflicts: `technical-dense` for reader-facing stories, `brutalist` for news trust surfaces, `retro-terminal` except niche publications, and `playful-rounded` for serious reporting.

## Often Forgotten States

- Updated or corrected article
- Paywall limit reached
- Offline saved article
- Media transcript unavailable
- Ad blocked layout gap
- End of article and next action
- Comment moderation pending
- Author/source unavailable

## Domain-Specific Failure Modes

- Article date appears without updated/corrected status
- Sponsored card is visually indistinguishable from editorial content
- Video autoplay steals focus or audio context
- Infinite feed loses reading position after returning from article
- Paywall appears after form entry without subscription terms
- Image caption or attribution is hidden on mobile
